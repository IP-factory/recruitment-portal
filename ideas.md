# Recruitment Portal — Visual Foundation

## Three possible directions

### 1. Evidence Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial, documentation-led interface that signals clear process, traceability, and sound judgement. It uses disciplined rules, quiet whitespace, and exact typographic hierarchy.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Quiet Authority
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained enterprise-SaaS system built around substantial navy anchors, soft white surfaces, and compact, readable component specimens. It is designed to feel mature, dependable, and easy to scale.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Studio Briefing
**Very Brief Intro:** A warmer professional-services direction with paper-like panels, a serif display accent, and considered editorial framing. It would feel consultancy-led rather than product-led.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction — Quiet Authority

### Design Movement
Contemporary enterprise software with the composure of a professional-services document system. The foundation should feel closer to a well-made internal standard than a conventional marketing landing page.

### Core Principles
1. **Structured calm:** Use clear sectional cadence, measured whitespace, and narrow horizontal rules to establish order without visual noise.
2. **Utility with restraint:** Every visual choice should clarify hierarchy, status, or interaction. Decoration is limited to the fine system grid and small typographic markers.
3. **Navy as an anchor:** The signature navy belongs to headings, action controls, and active information—not as a continuous page background.
4. **Reusable by default:** Components should look consistent whether they eventually belong in applicant, recruiter, or administrative workflows.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built from white and near-white working surfaces so that an applicant’s information always reads as the primary content. Deep navy (`#16263D`) gives the system authority; muted medium blue (`#436C9E`) gives links and selected states an approachable distinction. Status colours are deliberately desaturated to remain professional and never compete with workflow information.

### Layout Paradigm
An **asymmetrical specimen dossier**: a slim left rail frames the page and carries the page index, while the main canvas assembles wide functional sections in a vertical editorial sequence. Full-width labels and short divider lines mark each system group, replacing card-heavy dashboard grids.

### Signature Elements
1. A small navy **alignment mark**—two nested offset corner-lines used beside section labels and the simple logo lock-up.
2. **Index labels** in compact uppercase tracking, such as `01 / Navigation`, to make the page read like a shared design reference.
3. Fine **graph-paper guide lines** in the soft background of the foundation header, used sparingly and only in non-content areas.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are quiet and confident: controls tighten subtly on hover, focus states use a precise blue outline, and placeholders announce their future status with a short toast. The mobile menu and modal use quick, legible transitions instead of theatrical motion.

### Animation
Motion is limited to opacity and transform transitions under 220ms with a crisp ease-out. The mobile panel and modal may enter from `opacity: 0` and `scale(0.98)`; buttons compress to `scale(0.97)` on press. No looping animation, parallax, or animated decorative backgrounds. All nonessential motion is suppressed for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Inter** is the interface font throughout, as specified. Headings use Inter 600 with compact letter spacing; controls, table values, and body copy use Inter 400–500. Small uppercase labels use 600 weight and increased tracking. The type scale begins at 12px utility text, runs through 14–16px body text, and tops out at a 42px semibold H1.

### Brand Essence
**A recruitment-screening foundation for teams that need an orderly, credible way to assess candidates—built to make every step feel clear, not complex.**

**Personality:** dependable, exacting, calm.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be direct and systems-oriented. CTAs should name the next action precisely; microcopy should acknowledge useful limitations without sounding apologetic.

> “A clear foundation for considered hiring.”

> “Start an application when you are ready.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the requested **text placeholder**, “Recruitment Portal,” set in a distinct navy medium-weight wordmark. A minimal two-line navy alignment mark may sit before it as a tiny, non-elaborate visual anchor; the text remains the identity.

### Signature Brand Color
**Portal Navy — `#16263D`**

## Style Decisions

- The Portal Navy two-line alignment mark is the recurring identity motif. It appears next to the text wordmark, within sparse workspace markers, and beside empty or transitional states.
- Internal shell pages extend the same dossier language as the UI kit through compact uppercase context labels, decisive headings, and fine divider rules. This direction is applied without adding future dashboard content beyond the requested visual shell.
- Temporary copy remains explicitly grounded in the recruitment workflow when the specification permits it. The requested portal placeholder text remains intact because it is a mandatory foundation requirement.
